A little about how I work 🤍

“Come with an open heart, an open mind, and the willingness to see what the cards have to show you.”

Give your question some time to unfold.

I recommend not asking the same question again for at least 15 days. Returning too quickly can sometimes make a reading reflect what we want to hear rather than what we genuinely need to see.

This is a judgment-free space.

You can come as you are. My role is not to judge, criticise or tell you what you should have done. I’m here to hold space, offer clarity and help you look at your situation from a different perspective.

Some questions are outside the scope of Tarot.

I do not read on matters of birth or death. These are deeply personal and sacred matters that I choose not to interpret through Tarot.

Tarot is guidance, not a replacement for your choices.

The cards can offer perspective, possibilities and things to reflect on — but your life remains yours to navigate. Please use your own judgment and take real-world action where needed.

I will always be honest, with compassion.

I won’t tell you something simply because it sounds comforting. If the message is difficult, I’ll communicate it as gently and respectfully as I can.

There is always an energy exchange.

Readings are offered through a paid energy exchange. Free readings are not offered because the exchange honours the time, energy and intention that goes into this work.

Tarot Reading Guidelines

Please read before booking. By booking a session, you agree to these terms.

1. Booking & Payment
  • Sessions are fully prepaid.
  • Payment is non-refundable & non-transferable.
  • Your slot is confirmed only after payment.
2. Timing & Rescheduling
  • Please be available at your scheduled time.
  • 10-minute waiting time is allowed. After that, the session is considered completed.
  • One reschedule is allowed with 24 hours’ prior notice, within the same week.
3. Session Boundaries
  • Readings are limited to your booked session time.
  • Unlimited follow-ups, repeated questions and post-session emotional support are not included.
  • Additional guidance requires a new booking.
4. What I Don’t Read On

Health diagnosis • Pregnancy • Legal matters • Death • Black magic/negative rituals

Tarot offers guidance and perspective, not guaranteed outcomes.

5. Respect & Confidentiality
  • Please approach the reading with honesty and respect.
  • Manipulation, testing or disrespect may result in the session being ended without a refund.
  • Sessions are confidential. Screenshots or recordings cannot be shared without permission.
6. No Dependency

Tarot is here to support your clarity, not replace your own judgment or actions. Repeated readings on the same matter within a short period are discouraged.

7. Results & Free Will

Your choices, circumstances and timing influence outcomes. No reading can guarantee marriage, reunion, money or a specific timeline.

8. Boundaries

Personal messaging and emergency emotional support are not part of tarot services.

By booking, you confirm that you have read and agreed to these guidelines.
